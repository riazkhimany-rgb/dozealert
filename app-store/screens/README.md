## In App Store Connect

1. Open DozeAlert → your iOS version → **Screenshots** → **iPhone**.  
2. For **iPhone 6.5" Display**, drag files from `upload/6.5-inch/` in order 01 → 06 (add 07 if you want).  
3. If Connect also asks for **6.1"** or **6.7"**, use those folders the same way.  
4. Open the **Apple Watch** screenshot slot and upload the three files from `upload/watch-416x496/` (Ready → Watching → Idle).  
5. Click **Save**.

Portrait only. Do not use Wear OS screenshots for iPhone or Apple Watch slots.

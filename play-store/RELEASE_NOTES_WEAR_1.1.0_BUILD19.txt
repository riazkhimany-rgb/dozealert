Version 1.1.0 (build 100029) — Wear OS companion

• Richer trip display — next stop, direction label, GPS weak warnings, and route concern hints match the phone
• Tile & complication — refresh live data from the phone when you glance at your watch
• Start/stop parity — Start only when the phone trip is idle (no restart after a missed trip)
• Alarm dismiss — waits for the phone to confirm before closing, so alarms don’t re-fire
• Background sync — receives trip updates even when the phone UI is in the background

Requires DozeAlert 1.1.0 (build 19+) on your paired phone.

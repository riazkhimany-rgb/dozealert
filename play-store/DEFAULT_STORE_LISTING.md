## Full description (max 4,000 characters)

```
DozeAlert helps commuters sleep peacefully and arrive confidently.

Whether you're travelling by train, bus, car, or rideshare, DozeAlert monitors your trip and wakes you before you miss your destination.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick a transit stop or drop a pin on the map
• Start your trip — DozeAlert watches in the background
• Get a loud alarm and vibration when you're approaching your stop

FEATURES
• Bus, train, taxi, rideshare, and map-pin trips
• Transit Mode with stop-by-stop progress where stop lists are available
• Background monitoring with a persistent trip notification
• Adjustable wake distance so you arrive on time
• Map destination picker and place search
• My Trips: saved stops, saved lines, and past trip stats
• First-time setup guide with travel mode choice
• Agency stop lists where supported across Canada and the US
• Wear OS companion: trip status, start/stop, and alarm dismiss on a paired watch (tile and complication)
• Battery-conscious design for daily commutes and long trips

PERMISSIONS
DozeAlert uses location (including in the background while monitoring), notifications, physical activity on Android (to tell riding from waiting at a platform on transit trips), and optional battery optimization so alerts work when your screen is off. You control permissions in Android Settings.

No account required. No ads.

DozeAlert is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any transit agency.

Sleep peacefully. Arrive confidently.
```
